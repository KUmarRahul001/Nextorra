## 2. Retention Tactics & Churn Reduction

1. **Monthly Value Report:**
   - On the 1st of every month, clients receive an automated PDF summary: Uptime %, backups completed, security threats neutralized, speed score, and developer hours used.
2. **Rollover Hours:**
   - Up to 50% of unused developer hours can roll over into the next month, eliminating the feeling of "wasted subscription money".
3. **No Lock-In Contracts:**
   - Month-to-month billing with instant cancellation. Trust is built through performance, not hostage contracts.
