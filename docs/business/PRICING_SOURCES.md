## 1. Web Development & Digital Presence Benchmarks
1. **RedPulse Software India (2026 SME Pricing Study):**
   - Verified that standard brochure/business websites in India range from ₹15,000 to ₹90,000, with custom web applications starting from ₹1,50,000.
   - Reference: `https://redpulsesoftware.in`
2. **Urvija DigiGrowth & Apricorn Solutions (2026 India Market Quotes):**
   - Basic 1-5 page static websites start at ₹5,000 – ₹12,000 on low-end platforms, scaling to ₹40,000 – ₹80,000 for mid-tier agencies.
   - Reference: `https://urvijadigigrowth.com`, `https://apricornsolutions.com`
3. **Akoode & Deziti Tech Infrastructure Benchmarks:**
   - Evaluated ongoing hidden recurring costs in India: Domain ₹800–₹1,200/yr, Managed Hosting ₹2,500–₹6,000/yr, Security/AMC retainers ₹15,000–₹40,000/yr.
   - Reference: `https://akoode.com`, `https://deziti.com`
