## 2. Why Rahnoxa Stays Profitable at Low Prices

1. **Modular Architecture & Component Libraries:**
   - 70% of UI patterns (navigation, pricing tables, hero sections, forms, auth) are pre-engineered in our internal library. A 5-page site requires 10 hours of tailored execution rather than 50 hours of starting from scratch.
2. **Zero Sales Commission Overhead:**
   - Instead of paying high sales commissions or maintaining physical sales offices, customer acquisition relies on organic search, local listings, digital referrals, and word-of-mouth.
3. **Automated Triage & Scoping:**
   - RahBot pre-qualifies inquiries, collects specifications, and generates accurate project estimates automatically, reducing management overhead by over 80%.
