# 🚨 Rahnoxa Rescue & Last-Minute Saver Operating Model

**Core Brand Pillar:** The reliable technical emergency room for Indian businesses when everyone else is closed, slow, or unaffordable.

---

## 1. Why the "Rescue" Market is Wide Open in India

1. **The "Disappearing Freelancer" Phenomenon:**
   - In India, over 45% of small business website projects stall because the freelancer took an initial 50% advance and became unreachable or missed the launch date.
2. **Launch-Day Disasters:**
   - Marketing campaigns, festival sales (Diwali/New Year), exhibitions, and investor meetings have non-negotiable fixed deadlines.
3. **Agencies Refuse Small Fixes:**
   - Top agencies refuse to troubleshoot a broken contact form or SSL error because their minimum engagement threshold is ₹50,000.

---

## 2. Rahnoxa Rescue Triage Protocol (4-Step Workflow)

```
[Emergency Trigger] (WhatsApp / Web Form)
        ↓
1. Rapid Triage (SLA < 30 Minutes): Verify issue, check server logs, quote fixed price.
        ↓
2. Secure Access Handshake: Temporary scoped credentials (NDA + encrypted password exchange).
        ↓
3. Code / Server Intervention: Resolve error in staging, verify fix, backup database.
        ↓
4. Deploy & Handover: Push live, verify SSL/payments, deliver post-mortem report + credentials reset.
```

## 4. Operational Boundaries & Refusal Rules
To prevent burnout and maintain technical integrity, Rescue Services **will not** accept:
- Decrypting ransomware or repairing irrecoverably corrupted databases without backups.
- Bypassing two-factor authentication or gaining unauthorized access to third-party accounts.
- Same-day builds of full multi-tenant SaaS products (must be scoped as multi-week projects).
