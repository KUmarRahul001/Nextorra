# 👥 Indian SMB Customer Personas & Value Alignment

---

## Persona 1: "Doctor / Clinic Owner" (Dr. Rajiv Mehta)
- **Profile:** Runs a dental / multi-specialty clinic in Tier-2/Tier-1 city (e.g. Pune, Jaipur, Lucknow).
- **Core Problems:** Existing website is slow and not mobile-friendly; patients can’t find them on Google Maps; reception staff wastes hours taking appointment calls on WhatsApp.
- **Budget Tolerance:** ₹8,000 – ₹20,000 one-time, ₹2,000 – ₹4,000/month.
- **Primary Objections:** *"I don’t understand tech, will you handle everything?"*, *"Will my reception staff be able to use it?"*
- **Best Rahnoxa Offer:** **Standard Business Website (₹11,999)** + **Google Business Profile Domination (₹4,499)** + **WhatsApp Auto-Booking Bot (₹6,999)**.

---

## Persona 2: "The Solo Founder / Bootstrapped Startup" (Ananya Sharma)
- **Profile:** Building a B2B service / early SaaS product in Bangalore/Delhi. Has limited capital before raising seed funds.
- **Core Problems:** Needs an investor-ready landing page and functional MVP in 2 weeks; big agencies quoted ₹2,50,000 and 2 months.
- **Budget Tolerance:** ₹25,000 – ₹60,000 for MVP; urgent timeline.
- **Primary Objections:** *"Can you build this on React/Node so I don’t have to rewrite it later?"*, *"Can you sign an NDA?"*
- **Best Rahnoxa Offer:** **Express Landing Page (₹4,999)** + **React/Node MVP Foundation (₹39,999 – ₹69,999)**.

---

## Persona 3: "The Local Retailer / Boutique Shop" (Vikram Patel)
- **Profile:** Owns a clothing / jewelry / electronics store in Ahmedabad.
- **Core Problems:** Losing customers to Blinkit/Amazon; needs a digital catalog and direct WhatsApp ordering without paying 30% aggregator commissions.
- **Budget Tolerance:** ₹10,000 – ₹25,000.
- **Primary Objections:** *"I don’t want complex software that requires daily maintenance."*
- **Best Rahnoxa Offer:** **Digital Catalog & E-Commerce Store (₹14,999)** + **WhatsApp Catalog Integration (₹3,999)**.

---

## Persona 4: "The Panicked Marketer / Event Organizer" (Rohan Verma)
- **Profile:** Marketing manager running a product launch or conference in 3 days.
- **Core Problems:** The freelance developer disappeared, registration form is broken, and ads go live on Friday.
- **Budget Tolerance:** ₹5,000 – ₹20,000 (Values speed and certainty above all else).
- **Primary Objections:** *"Can you guarantee this will be live and tested by tomorrow 6 PM?"*
- **Best Rahnoxa Offer:** **Emergency Website & Bug Rescue (₹2,999 – ₹7,499)** or **Express 24-Hour Landing Page (₹7,499)**.
