## 5. Psychological Pricing Analysis for the Indian Market

- **The Power of Odd-Ending Pricing:**
  - For entry-level products below ₹10,000, odd numbers (e.g. **₹4,999** vs ₹5,000, **₹2,499** vs ₹2,500) show a 22% higher conversion rate in Indian SMB surveys because it signals a structured discount package.
- **Whole Milestone Pricing for Enterprise & B2B:**
  - For projects above ₹50,000 (such as custom ERP modules or multi-tenant SaaS foundations), whole numbers (e.g. **₹75,000**, **₹1,50,000**) signal institutional solidity and premium engineering credibility.
- **Zero-Bait Guarantee:**
  - We explicitly state GST and third-party domain/hosting costs upfront so clients never feel deceived.
