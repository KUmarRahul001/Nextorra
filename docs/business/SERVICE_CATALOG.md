## 2. Comprehensive Service Modules

### Pillar A: Web & Digital Presence

#### A1. Express Landing Page (Event / Ad / Lead Generation)
- **Target Customer:** Event organizers, course creators, real estate agents, product launches.
- **Deliverables:** Single-page high-conversion responsive design, hero section, benefits, social proof/testimonials, lead capture form, WhatsApp click-to-chat button, Google Analytics & Meta Pixel integration.
- **Turnaround:** Standard: 3 Days | Express: 24 Hours.

#### A2. Essential Business Website (3–5 Pages)
- **Target Customer:** Local service businesses, clinics, accounting firms, consultancies, coaching centers.
- **Deliverables:** Modern multi-page architecture (Home, About, Services, Portfolio/Gallery, Contact), mobile-optimized responsive layout, on-page SEO meta tags, Google Maps embed, SSL setup, contact form with email notifications.
- **Turnaround:** Standard: 5–7 Days | Fast: 3 Days.

#### A3. Dynamic Business Platform & Catalog (6–10 Pages)
- **Target Customer:** Manufacturers, wholesale distributors, specialized clinics, multi-service companies.
- **Deliverables:** Interactive product catalog, advanced filtering, dynamic inquiry cart, custom contact workflows, customer testimonials slider, blog/insights engine.
- **Turnaround:** Standard: 10–14 Days | Fast: 6 Days.

#### A4. E-Commerce Starter Store (D2C & Retail)
- **Target Customer:** Direct-to-consumer brands, fashion boutiques, local specialty stores.
- **Deliverables:** Complete product catalog setup (up to 30 items), category navigation, Razorpay / Cashfree payment gateway integration, shipping calculator, automated WhatsApp order receipts.
- **Turnaround:** Standard: 10–14 Days.

- **SLA:** Triage & Action Plan within 24 Hours.

## 3. Services Rahnoxa Will NOT Offer (Strategic Exclusions)

To protect delivery velocity, high profit margins, and team focus, Rahnoxa explicitly avoids:

1. **₹999 Template Installations:** Destroys brand equity and attracts high-churn, low-value clients who consume excessive support.
2. **Cold Calling Telemarketing / Bulk Spam Services:** Regulatory risk, low conversion, high reputational hazard.
3. **Black-Hat SEO Link Farms:** High risk of Google manual penalties that harm customer domains.
4. **Custom Mobile Games / High-End 3D Animation:** Massive capital overhead and distraction from core business software.
5. **On-Premises Hardware / Networking Support:** Geographically restricted and low margin.
