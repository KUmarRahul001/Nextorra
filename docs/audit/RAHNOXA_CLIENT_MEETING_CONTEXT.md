# RAHNOXA — CLIENT MEETING CONTEXT & QUOTATION PLAYBOOK

This document is engineered to guide client discovery calls, proposal drafting, and quotation calculations.

---

1. **Modern, Fast Web Experiences**: Built on React 18, Vite, and Tailwind CSS — achieving sub-second load times and 90+ Google PageSpeed scores.
2. **Custom Full-Stack Capabilities**: Production-grade Node.js/Express APIs backed by Supabase PostgreSQL — no brittle no-code workarounds.

4. **Complete Source Code & IP Handover**: 100% intellectual property ownership transferred to the client upon final payment. No vendor lock-in.
5. **Post-Launch Peace of Mind**: A 30-day bug warranty included with every delivery at no additional charge.

## 2. What RAHNOXA Must NOT Claim (Overclaim Guardrails)
* **Do NOT claim to be a payment gateway**: State: *"We build seamless Razorpay, Stripe, or Cashfree integrations directly into your platform using your business merchant account."*
* **Do NOT promise unlimited free phone calling**: State: *"Our AI voice qualification runs in-browser out-of-the-box. For cellular/GSM outbound dialer campaigns, we connect your preferred SIP trunk (Airtel, Tata, Twilio)."*
* **Do NOT present prices below the commercial floor**: Full multi-page business websites start at **₹5,000**. Never compromise below this floor.

---

## 3. Mandatory Client Discovery Questions (Before Quoting)

1. **Business Objective**: Is this site meant for lead generation, brand credibility, online sales, or internal business operations?
2. **Page Count & Content**: How many unique pages or views do you require? Do you have ready text and images, or do you need content assistance?
3. **Dynamic Features**: Does the site require an admin panel to update projects/blogs, a client login area, or simple static presentation?
4. **Third-Party Integrations**: Do you need WhatsApp click-to-chat, payment gateways (Razorpay), CRM webhooks, or automated email/SMS notifications?
5. **Hosting & Domain**: Do you already own your domain and hosting, or should RAHNOXA manage deployment setup (Cloudflare, Render, Supabase)?
6. **Launch Timeline**: What is your firm deadline for going live?

---

## 4. Standard Commercial & Payment Terms
* **50% Advance Commitment**: Required prior to kick-off to allocate engineering resources.
* **50% Balance on Delivery**: Payable upon staging approval on our preview server, prior to live domain release and repository transfer.
* **Revision Policy**: 2 complete rounds of design/layout revisions included in standard proposals.
* **Bug Fix Warranty**: 30 days post-launch warranty covering broken functionality or layout defects.
